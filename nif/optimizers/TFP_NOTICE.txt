Modifications (2026): restricted to unbatched eager real f32/f64 scalar control
flow; Torch tensor arithmetic and optimizer interface; explicit validation,
safe checkpoint schema, per-step additional budget and cumulative counters;
closure-exception parameter cleanup; no TensorFlow dependency, graph tracing,
batching, meta-differentiation, or claim of backend bitwise equivalence.
